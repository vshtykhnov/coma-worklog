How to log time in Jira faster

Jira logs time one entry at a time. That's fine for a single task and slow for a whole week. These are the ways to cut it down, from small habits to drafting the week at once.

The built-in way: Log work

In Jira Cloud, time goes on each work item separately:

  1. Open the work item.
  2. Select More actions (•••) → Log work, or click the Time tracking field.
  3. Enter the time spent, the date, an optional comment, and save.

If you don't see Time tracking, it is switched off for your space. Only a Jira admin can turn it on, and you need the Work on work items permission to log time.

Three things make this slow when you log after the fact:

  • One work item and one entry per dialog, so a week of work means a dozen dialogs.
  • You have to remember what you worked on and for how long, task by task.
  • Jira has no built-in weekly view for entering time. Timesheets usually come from a Marketplace app your admin installs.

Make the native flow quicker

Type time the way Jira reads it

Jira accepts 1h 30m, 45m or 1d. A day and a week mean however many hours your admin set as a working day and week, so check before you type 1d.

Open Log work from the keyboard

Press Ctrl + K (Cmd + K on Mac) on a work item to open Jira's command palette, then search for Log work.

Log daily, not monthly

The slow part is reconstructing what you did. Two minutes at the end of each day beats an hour of guessing at the end of the month.

Draft the whole week at once

Your Jira history already knows most of the answer: which tasks you moved to In Progress, how long they stayed there and who they were assigned to. Snaplog is a Chrome extension that turns that history into worklog drafts in a calendar:

  1. Select the days you need, a single day or a full week.
  2. Click Suggest worklogs. Empty days fill with drafts; days you already logged stay as they are.
  3. Fix anything that looks off, then Submit posts every draft to Jira.

The entries are ordinary Jira worklogs, the same as Log work creates, so Jira's own reports see them. Connecting Google Calendar or Outlook adds meetings, and GitHub adds code reviews.

When this isn't the right fit

  • Snaplog works with Jira Cloud in Chrome; Jira Data Center isn't supported.
  • Suggestions are estimates from status history, not measured time. Review them before you submit.
  • Some companies block browser extensions or require approved time-tracking apps.
  • If your team needs timesheet approvals or billing reports, a full timesheet app fits better. See how Snaplog compares with Tempo.
