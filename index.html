The main idea

Worklogs without the daily bookkeeping.

01

Select your days

Drag across consecutive days or Ctrl/Cmd-click separate dates in the calendar.

02

Let Snaplog suggest

Empty days fill with drafts from Jira status history. Days you already logged stay as they are.

03

Review, then post

One Submit posts the whole week. Nothing goes to Jira until you say so.

Fast manual edits

One change, several days.

Select any combination of days and add the same task to all of them. Drag a selection across a range, or drag an entry to another day when plans change.

Edit and post

Fix a draft, post the week.

Click any entry to change the task, time or comment. Submit sends every draft to Jira at once, and posted entries get a check mark.

Optional, but handy

The rest of your day counts too.

Meetings

Google Calendar or Outlook

Meetings you attended become drafts on the Jira task you pick once. Read-only access.

Code reviews

GitHub

Reviewed pull requests become a daily review draft, sized by your active time on the PR page.

Reminder

A red icon, not a nag

The toolbar icon turns red when past workdays have no time in Jira. Set the threshold or turn it off.

Minimal by design

Just the worklogs. None of the clutter.

No dashboards to configure. No timers to babysit. No Snaplog server either: your Jira data goes from your browser straight to Jira.

The short version

Before you install

Does Snaplog track my activity?

No. Suggestions are estimates from Jira status history, meetings and reviews. The only timer is optional: active time on GitHub pull request pages, kept in your browser.

Does it post automatically?

No. Every suggestion remains a draft until you submit it.

What does it support?

Jira Cloud in Chrome. Google Calendar, Outlook and GitHub are optional. Your organization may still restrict extensions or connected services.
